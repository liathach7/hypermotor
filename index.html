Car physics

To simulate fuel efficiency(FE) real-world driving data was taken from Envirocar. This is a public database where drivers have used OBD readers to upload data from their trips. I used throttle position(0-100%), speed(km/h), instantaneous fuel consumption(l/h) and latitude and longitudes. These are recorded at 5s intervals.


Graphs

I plotted throttle position against acceleration.

There is a correlation between throttle position and acceleration but not a linear trend. However when I separated the speeds into cohorts I saw a clearer trend. E.g. here is when the speeds are 10-30km/h..

I could now draw a line so the acceleration is calculated based on throttle position. Similarly I plotted throttle position(TP) against fuel consumption to calculate fuel consumption based on TP.

What is the deceleration when the throttle is off? I plotted speed vs. acceleration when the throttle was less than 4% (effectively 0)

Looking at the points where acceleration is less than zero, it's clear the higher the starting speed the greater the deceleration.
